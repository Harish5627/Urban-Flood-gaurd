
# 🌧️ Urban FloodGuard
### AI-Powered Urban Flood Risk Prediction and Monitoring System

## 📌 Project Overview

Urban FloodGuard is a smart-city project designed to identify potentially flood-prone urban areas during heavy rainfall. It combines rainfall information, geographical location, terrain characteristics, and water-level data to estimate flood risk and display potentially affected areas on an interactive dashboard.

The system aims to help citizens understand local flood risks and support city authorities in making faster, data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Urban areas frequently experience waterlogging and flooding during intense rainfall. Citizens and emergency response teams may not receive timely, location-specific information about unsafe streets and flood-prone areas.

Urban FloodGuard aims to address this challenge by providing an interactive platform for visualizing flood risk and monitoring relevant environmental conditions.

## 💡 Proposed Solution

Urban FloodGuard provides a centralized dashboard to:

- Visualize flood-risk levels across locations.
- Display rainfall and weather information.
- Simulate rainfall scenarios to understand potential flood risks.
- Allow citizens to submit flood and waterlogging reports.
- Help identify potentially vulnerable areas.
- Support faster and more informed emergency planning.

## ✨ Key Features

- 🗺️ **Interactive Dashboard:** View flood-risk information by location.
- 🌦️ **Weather Monitoring:** Display weather and rainfall information.
- ⚠️ **Flood Risk Classification:** Categorize areas into risk levels such as Low, Medium, and High.
- 🌧️ **Rainfall Simulation:** Explore how changing rainfall conditions may affect risk.
- 📍 **Location Search:** Search for an area to view relevant information.
- 📝 **Citizen Reports:** Collect reports about waterlogging and flooding.
- 🤖 **AI/ML Integration:** Designed to support machine-learning-based flood-risk prediction.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

1. **Data Collection:** Obtain rainfall, weather, location, terrain, and water-level information.
2. **Data Processing:** Clean and prepare the collected data.
3. **Risk Analysis:** Apply defined rules or a trained machine-learning model to estimate flood risk.
4. **Visualization:** Display risk categories and relevant information on the dashboard.
5. **Citizen Feedback:** Collect local reports to help identify waterlogging incidents.
6. **Decision Support:** Present risk information to support public awareness and emergency planning.

## ⚠️ Limitations

This project is a prototype for flood-risk awareness and decision support. Risk estimates depend on the quality and availability of the underlying data and prediction methods.

Sample data or simulated results must not be treated as verified live flood predictions. The system is not a replacement for official weather alerts, emergency instructions, or government flood-warning services.
